Executive Summary

In the 26D release of the Maintenance module, several new features have been introduced to enhance operational efficiency. Notable capabilities include the ability to associate technicians with multiple labor resources, add parts from asset parts lists to work orders, and manage multiple work orders using mass actions. Additionally, users can now configure additional fields for asset details and capture quality events in Service Logistics. Customers are required to perform pre-update and post-update tasks, and they can opt in to new features that are delivered disabled. It's essential to review the specific privileges needed to enable these features and ensure a smooth transition to the new functionalities.
