Executive Summary

The 26D release of Order Management brings a host of new capabilities and enhancements aimed at improving supply chain efficiency and pricing management. Key features include support for intra-organizational material transfers in Supply Chain Orchestration, which enhances fulfillment flexibility, and a redesigned user interface for managing pricing messages and parameters. Additionally, the release emphasizes the importance of pre-update tasks and the option to opt into new features, ensuring that customers can leverage the latest enhancements effectively. Users are encouraged to review the new functionalities and prepare for necessary actions to maximize their benefits.

What's New

  • Supply Chain Orchestration now supports intra-organizational material transfers for supply requests from Self Service Procurement, improving fulfillment flexibility and inventory utilization. (Enable intra-organizational material transfers for transfer supply requests originating from Self Service Procurement)
  • Oracle Supply Chain Orchestration captures inventory on hand striping attributes for manual transfer supply requests, enhancing fulfillment precision. (Inventory on hand striping attributes for manual transfer supply requests in Supply Chain Orchestration)
  • Purchase requisitions from min-max planning and PAR replenishment can now be routed through the Oracle Self Service Procurement approval workflow, streamlining the replenishment process. (Route inventory min-max planning and PAR replenishment purchase requests through procurement approval workflow)
  • Minimum remaining shelf life support is now available in back-to-back fulfillment flows, ensuring inventory meets shelf life requirements during transfers. (Support minimum remaining shelf life for back-to-back transfer and on hand flows)
  • Collaboration Messaging allows deriving the pay site for GHX inbound invoices using B2B Account Number setup, ensuring correct invoice routing. (Derive supplier site from invoice account numbers)
  • Organizations in Brazil can now receive additional electronic fiscal documents through Oracle Collaboration Messaging, enhancing compliance with local regulations. (Receive Brazil electronic fiscal invoices for services and utilities)
  • Oracle Subscription Management can now transform potential subscriptions into renewal quotes in Oracle CPQ, automating notifications about processing status. (Receive a renewal quote from Oracle Subscription Management)
  • A new capability allows users to receive sales contracts from Oracle CPQ directly into Oracle Enterprise Contracts Management, facilitating streamlined contract management. (Receive a sales contract from Oracle CPQ)
  • Collaboration Messaging can now be accessed from Redwood pages while maintaining the classic configuration model, improving user experience. (Use Collaboration Messaging in Redwood experience with classic flows)
  • The system now enforces minimum remaining shelf life requirements during order fulfillment, enhancing compliance and customer satisfaction. (Enforce Minimum Remaining Shelf Life in Order Fulfillment)
  • Users can create custom SQL queries as user-defined queries against the Read-Optimized Data Store, enhancing data extraction capabilities. (Support for user-defined queries in data extracts)
  • A new user interface for authoring and negotiating rate plans with entitlements has been introduced, allowing seamless management of charges within subscriptions. (Author and negotiate rate plan with entitlements)
  • Pricing administrators can now import large numbers of rate plans using file-based data import, streamlining the management of subscription pricing. (Import large numbers of rate plans for price lists)
  • The system now supports parallel runs for collecting and loading planning data processes during targeted refresh collections, improving throughput and scalability. (Enable parallel runs for load data process during targeted refresh collections)
  • Supplier claims in the Settlement Pending status will automatically reopen when the corresponding Payables invoice is canceled, maintaining accounting consistency. (Reopen settlement pending supplier claims when payables invoices are canceled)

What's Changed

  • Oracle Global Order Promising now respects expiration dates of specific on-hand quantities in material reservations, enhancing the reservation netting process. (Respect expiration dates of specific on-hand quantities in material reservations)
  • The Pricing Messages page has been redesigned to improve management efficiency while retaining existing functionality. (Create and manage pricing messages)
  • The Pricing Parameters page has been redesigned for a modern setup experience, allowing users to search and update parameter values more efficiently. (Manage pricing parameters)
