Executive Summary

In the 26D release of Product Lifecycle Management, several new features enhance functionality across the module. Notable additions include decimal quantity support in the Configurator, allowing for fractional quantities, and the ability to define customer-specific inspection plans in Quality Management. The update also introduces a Quality Assurance Workspace for better analysis of inspection results and a new interface for managing import batches. Customers are required to complete pre-update tasks and can opt into new features to fully leverage these enhancements. Additionally, there are important considerations regarding the transition to Redwood pages that users should review.
