Executive Summary

In the 26D release for Manufacturing, several new features and enhancements have been introduced across various modules, particularly in Supply Chain Orchestration and Quality Management. Notable additions include support for intra-organizational material transfers, which enhances fulfillment flexibility, and the ability to define customer inspection plans for better quality management. Clients are required to perform pre-update and post-update tasks and can opt into new features that may be disabled by default. Additionally, the introduction of electronic signatures for quality inspections and dispensing instructions strengthens compliance and traceability in manufacturing processes.

What's New

  • Supply Chain Orchestration now supports intra-organizational material transfers for supply requests from Self Service Procurement, allowing users to receive supply source recommendations from multiple organizations. (Enable intra-organizational material transfers for transfer supply requests originating from Self Service Procurement)
  • Quality Management introduces the ability to define customer inspection plans and compare inspection results against these plans, improving product quality management. (Define customer inspection plans and compare inspection results)
  • Operators can perform work in process inspections using industrial handheld devices, enhancing efficiency and accuracy in quality management. (Inspect work in process using industrial handheld devices)
  • The Quality Assurance Workspace provides a centralized location for quality teams to analyze inspection results and monitor quality risks, improving decision-making. (Quality Assurance Workspace)
  • Manufacturers can now define and execute parallel operations on discrete production lines, improving operational efficiency and reducing lead times. (Define and execute parallel operations on discrete production lines)
  • New features in Process Manufacturing include the ability to create dispensing instructions that operators must acknowledge before dispensing materials, enhancing compliance. (Create dispensing instructions and enforce operator compliance)
  • Production campaigns can now be executed for batches, allowing planners to group and sequence compatible batches while considering resource calendars and changeovers. (Execute production campaigns for batches)
  • The system now requires electronic signatures for dispensing instruction confirmations, ensuring compliance and traceability in dispensing operations. (Verify dispensing instructions using Electronic Records and Electronic Signatures)
  • Users can now create user-defined queries for data extracts, allowing for custom SQL queries against the Read-Optimized Data Store. (Support for user-defined queries in data extracts)

What's Changed

  • Purchase requisitions from min-max planning and PAR replenishment can now be automatically routed through the procurement approval workflow, streamlining the replenishment process. (Route inventory min-max planning and PAR replenishment purchase requests through procurement approval workflow)
  • The capability to capture parent lot information has been enhanced across various production transactions, improving validation and search functionality. (Capture parent lot and lot information for production transactions)
  • Work order execution time tracking has been enhanced to include individual operator participation, improving productivity analysis and resource usage traceability. (Track work order execution time for individual operators)

Deprecations, Retirements and Replacements

None identified in this release.

  • Clients should opt in to new features delivered in this release, which may be enabled or disabled by default. Use the Navigator to access My Enterprise > New Features to enable desired features. (Optional Uptake of New Features (Opt In))
