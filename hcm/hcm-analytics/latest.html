Executive Summary

Release 26.R2 of HCM Analytics brings substantial enhancements across multiple modules within Fusion Data Intelligence. Key updates include the introduction of Peer Benchmark dynamic data visualizations and a People Leader Workbench in Oracle Fusion HCM Analytics, which facilitate better analysis of employee trends.
