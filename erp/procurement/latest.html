Executive Summary

The 26D release for Procurement and Self Service Procurement brings a wealth of new capabilities aimed at enhancing operational efficiency and collaboration. Key highlights include the ability to enable intra-organizational material transfers, improved inventory management with attributes for manual transfers, and streamlined procurement approval workflows. Additionally, new REST resources facilitate better integration with external systems, while enhancements to the user experience in both Procurement and Self Service Procurement modules aim to simplify processes. Customers are encouraged to review and opt in to new features, as some require specific configurations to be fully utilized.
