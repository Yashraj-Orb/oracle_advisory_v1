Executive Summary

Release 26.R2 of Oracle Fusion Data Intelligence brings a host of enhancements across multiple analytics modules, including ERP, HCM, SCM, and CX Analytics. Key updates include the introduction of Peer Benchmark visualizations and a People Leader Workbench in HCM Analytics, as well as a new Service Work Order Subject Area in CX Analytics. Additionally, ERP Analytics now allows configuration of the Earliest Extract Date for data extraction, while SCM Analytics adds the Demand Fulfillment subject area for improved supply chain planning. Customers should note that a mandatory patch is included to address compatibility issues, ensuring a smooth transition to this release.
